# Trip Radar 0.31.0 gap audit (branch `devam`, 2026-10-05)

Method: `npm run build`, then the built extension in Chrome for Testing (headless) with the demo trip, via
throwaway scripts `/private/tmp/claude-501/audit/audit*.mjs`. Screenshots are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/audit/shots/`
(live `1440-*` / `560-*`, mockups `ref-*-1440.png` / `ref-*-560.png`). DOM facts are in `dom-facts.json`.
Tags: **BUG** (wrong behaviour), **MISSING** (not built), **DIFFERENT** (built, but not as designed).

Before the list, two premises are wrong:
- **Complaint 2 (hover ×) is not in any approved design.** ulasim-v3, etkinlik-v4 and the spec put delete in
  `•••` → Sil and in the opened card's "Sil", with no confirm dialog and an 8 s "Geri al". That part is built
  and works (`1440-11-undo-toast.png`). A hover × is a new request, not a missed one.
- **Complaint 5 (Fikirler page) was scoped out on purpose.** `plan-kartlari-design.md` → "Kapsam dışı
  (sonraki sürüm): Fikirler sekmesi (restoranlar ve yapılacaklar; onaylı v1 taslağındaki tasarım)". So it is
  not built (true), but 0.31.0 did not claim it. It is still a real gap against the approved fikirler-v1 mockup.

## A. Hero (hero-v8)

Mostly faithful: photo with city switcher, countdown + route tab, 40 px title, stats box, "Seni böyle
anladım" row, facts column with budget bar, next step and the quiet line (`1440-01-hero.png`, `560-01-hero.png`).

1. **DIFFERENT: "Yerel" never shows the exchange rate for a TR traveller budgeting in EUR.** The design shows
   "€1 = ₺38,20 · −2 saat". Live shows only "−2 saat". Cause: `src/app/TripPanel.tsx:219` passes
   `homeCurrency: decisions?.ctx.currency` (the trip's budget currency, EUR) to `src/lib/tripFacts.ts:56`, so
   EUR→EUR is skipped. Fix: use the traveller's home currency (from the Settings passport: TR→TRY, or a
   Settings field) as `homeCurrency`, and keep the budget currency only for the budget row.
2. **DIFFERENT: "Sıradaki adım" can be a label instead of an action.** The design shows an imperative
   ("Madeira konaklamasını seç →"). Live shows "İptal süresi: Lisboa Loft" because `src/app/TripFacts.tsx:92`
   prints `kinds()` label + title, and a deadline todo sorts first (`src/app/Progress.tsx:38`). Fix: give each
   todo kind a verb phrase ("Lisboa Loft'u iptal etmeden karar ver", "Karar ver: Porto konaklama").
3. **DIFFERENT (minor): ground colours.** The mockup uses white and `#f5f5f7` grey boxes. The live hero sits on
   the app's warm beige, so the stats box and facts column are beige-grey. The spec puts "lavanta zeminin
   uygulamaya yayılması" out of scope, so this is a known gap. Fix only if wanted: set `.hx-stats` and
   `.hx-side` to `#f5f5f7` (`static/app.css`).

## B. Transport cards (ulasim-v3, with the etkinlik-v4 top line the spec adopted)

4. **BUG (complaint 1, confirmed live): a flight is drawn as a taxi once its airport transfer gets a mode.**
   Reproduced: in Günlük akış, day 1 "Havalimanı → Otel" → 🚕 Taksi. Back on Plan, the Pegasus flight card
   says "Taksi · transfer" with a taxi silhouette between IST and OPO (`1440-16-flight-as-taxi.png`;
   `dom-facts.json` "cards after taxi").
   The cause has two parts, both as suspected:
   - `src/lib/cardKinds.ts` `legModeByItem` maps `[...leg.options, ...leg.travel.items]` to `leg.choice.mode`.
     For an arrival or departure transfer, `leg.travel` is the flight or train it meets
     (`src/lib/legs.ts` `local(...)`, `arriving`/`departing` pass `travel`). So the flight inherits the
     transfer's "taxi".
   - In `src/lib/legs.ts:349-356` the `departing` transfer is pushed before the `move`. Because of
     `if (!out.has(i.id))`, the transfer's mode also wins over the move's own mode. A train or flight between
     cities turns into a taxi as soon as "Otel → Gar" gets one.
   - `transportMode()` checks the leg mode before `category === "flight"`. That follows the spec's order, so
     the spec order is part of the cause.
   Fix: in `legModeByItem`, map only `leg.options`, and `leg.travel.items` only when `leg.kind === "move"`.
   In `transportMode`, return "flight" for `category === "flight"` before looking at the leg. Add a unit test:
   arrival leg with taxi → its flight stays "flight"; departing leg with taxi → the move's train stays "train".
5. **DIFFERENT: the big text at each end is an airport code or station name, not the city.** The design shows
   "Lizbon" big with "LIS · **08.15**" small, and "Porto" big with "Campanhã · **09.30**" small. Live shows
   "IST" / "OPO" big with "8 Ekim · 07:10" small, and "Porto Campanhã" / "Lisboa Santa Apolónia" big.
   Cause: `src/lib/cardView.ts` `transportFace()` uses `f.from`/`f.to` as `city` and the day as `sub`. Fix: big
   = city (`cityOfAirport(code)` or the leg's city), sub = code/station · time. The day is already on the top line.
6. **BUG: long station names run into the silhouette.** "Porto Campanhã" and "Lisboa Santa Apolónia" at
   30 px overlap the train drawing at 1440 (`1440-05-hover-between.png`, `1440-02-plan.png`). Cause: `.pk-art`
   is absolutely centred over 46 % of the width (`static/app.css:1138`), and `.pk-mid` end cells don't keep
   clear of it (`:1174`). Fix: #5 removes most of it. Also let the end names wrap inside
   `(100% − 170px) / 2` and drop to 24 px when they are long.
7. **DIFFERENT (minor): transfer cards have no document slot and no Sil.** The spec says "Belge erişimi" and
   "Sil" on every card. `src/app/cards/LegCard.tsx` passes no `docs` and its menu has only "Gerek yok" /
   "Planı temizle". So a transfer voucher can't be attached to an airport taxi set from the leg. Fix: when
   the leg's settled item exists (`legItem(leg)`), show `DocAccess` for it and add "Sil" in its menu.

## C. Activity / eSIM / insurance media cards (etkinlik-v4)

Ring, kind label, date · time, paperclip / doc pill, 176×128 picture (dotted silhouette when there is no photo),
20 px title, info line, 48 px foot, "Bileti aldım" are all there (`1440-06-expanded-activity.png`,
`560-15-card-1.png`). The demo has no photos or ratings, so the photo + score corner and the ★ line could not be
checked live. `mediaFace` builds them (`src/lib/cardView.ts`).

8. **DIFFERENT: the foot's status line is cut with "…".** Douro shows "Seçildi · ⏳ Bilet alınmadı · etkinliğe
   4 gün · ücretsiz iptalli, şimdi a…". DESIGN.md says "Kart satırları kesilmez ('…' yok)". The design's foot
   is short ("Seçildi · bilet alınmadı"). Cause: `.pk-state { text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap }`
   (`static/app.css:1210`), and `footOf()` swaps the sub for the long `dateAlert` text
   (`src/lib/cardView.ts` `withAlert`). Fix: keep the foot sub short ("bilet alınmadı · 4 gün"), and move the
   full alert sentence to the detail panel, where it is already repeated.
9. **DIFFERENT: the `•••` menu has no "Gerek yok (gizle)" for records.** The spec's menu is "Düzenle · Gerek yok ·
   Ele · Sil". `menuFor()` (`src/lib/cardView.ts`) returns only edit / dismiss / delete. A chosen page item
   gets only "Sil" (`dom-facts.json` "menu items": ["Sil"]). Fix: add "Gerek yok" (hide via `trip.hidden`) for
   records that are no booking (taxi, note, to-do), or record in the spec that it is dropped.

## D. Add / delete (ulasim-v3 `.insert`, "Ekle" sheet, "Silince" toast; complaints 2 and 3)

10. **MISSING (complaint 2, new request): no hover × on plan cards.** Hovering Douro shows only the paperclip,
    `•••` and "Bileti aldım" (`1440-04-hover-card.png`; `dom-facts.json` "x button on hovered card"). The
    existing delete (••• → Sil, detail → Sil, 8 s undo, files restored) works
    (`src/app/cards/PlanCard.tsx` `menu`, `src/lib/removal.ts`, `src/lib/undo.ts` UNDO_MS = 8000).
    Fix: in `CardShell` (`src/app/cards/CardShell.tsx`), add a `.pk-x` button in `.pk-end`, shown on
    `.pk-card:hover` / `:focus-within`, that calls the same `env.remove(item)`. Pass it only for item cards
    (PlanCard, SwipeCard), not for leg cards. Update ulasim-v3 / DESIGN.md "Azaltmak" so the design records it.
11. **BUG: removing a stay or a planned stay has no "Geri al".** `SwipeCard.tsx:302` ("Kaldır") and `:318`
    ("Planı kaldır") call `removeItem()` → `deleteItem()` (`src/app/actions.ts:66`) without `undo.show`. So the
    stay and its files are gone with no toast. Fix: route them through the card env's `remove` (`env.remove`),
    as PlanCard does.
12. **BUG: the drawer's delete still uses `confirm()` and has no undo.** `src/app/ItemDrawer.tsx:157-158`
    contradicts "Onay penceresi yok; 8 saniyelik Geri al". Fix: call the same undo-backed remove and close.
13. **MISSING: page-saved stays have no Sil at all.** Jardim / Ribeira / Casa Azul offer only "Ele" / "Seç" /
    "Detaylar" (`1440-02-plan.png`). The spec says "••• → Sil her kartta". Fix: add `•••` with Ele / Sil to
    `SwipeCard` and `SettledCard`.
14. **BUG (complaint 3): the "+" pre-fills an airport code or station as the city.** Opening the sheet from
    each "+" on the demo gives "OPO · 8 Ekim", "Porto · 8 Ekim", "Porto · 9 Ekim",
    "Lisboa Santa Apolónia · 11 Ekim", "Lizbon · 11 Ekim", "IST · 14 Ekim". Expected: Porto, Porto, Porto,
    Lizbon, Lizbon, and "İstanbul" or nothing for the last. Cause: `src/lib/templates.ts` `insertAt()` case
    `"travel"` uses `flight.to` before `entry.leg?.to.city`. Fix: prefer `entry.leg?.to.city`, then
    `cityOfAirport(flight.to)`. After the return flight, use no city.
15. **MISSING (complaint 3): no "+" before the first entry or at the top of a city block.** There is exactly
    one `InsertPoint` per row, after it (`src/app/Timeline.tsx:193`; 6 rows, 6 points). So nothing can be put
    before "Varış" (e.g. a taxi to IST airport) or at the start of a city block. Fix: render an `InsertPoint`
    before the first row of `.trip-line` and at the head of each `city-block`, with
    `{ city: block.city, date: range.start }`.
16. **DIFFERENT (complaint 3): no way to aim at a day inside a stay.** The plan has no day rows. The "+" after
    a stay pre-fills its check-in day ("Porto · 8 Ekim" after a stay of 8–11 Ekim), and the "+" after Douro
    gives 9 Ekim. Nothing points at 10 Ekim. Günlük akış, which has days, has no "+" at all
    (`dom-facts.json` "insert points in days view": 0). Fix: add a "+" to each Günlük akış day header and empty
    day ("boş gün"), pre-filled with that day and its city. On Plan, after a stay, pre-fill the city and leave
    the date for the user.
17. **DIFFERENT (complaint 3): the "+" is hard to find.** It is invisible (`opacity: 0`) until the pointer is
    on a 22 px strip under the card, only in the right column (`static/app.css:1263-1266`;
    `1440-05-hover-between.png`). On touch it never shows. The mockup has the same hover rule, so this is as
    designed. Still, it is why "+" feels missing. Fix: show the dashed line and "+" at low opacity (~.35) all
    the time, full on hover, and always visible below 620 px.
18. **DIFFERENT (minor): the restaurant / activity form has no city field.** "Restoran" from a "+" shows only
    Ad / Tarih / Fiyat (`1440-10-add-form-prefilled.png`). The city is carried silently from the "+", so from
    the header "+ Ekle" a restaurant is saved with no city. The spec's form ("Ad, Tarih, Fiyat") allows this,
    but the item can't find its city block. Fix: add an optional "Şehir" field to the `named` form
    (`src/app/cards/AddSheet.tsx`), pre-filled from `at.city`.

## E. To-dos vs bookable activities, and the Fikirler page (fikirler-v1; complaints 4 and 5)

19. **MISSING (complaint 4): a to-do and a bookable activity can't be told apart.** Every `category: "activity"`
    item becomes `cardKind "activity"` (`src/lib/cardKinds.ts` `cardKind`), gets ticket words "Plana seç →
    Bileti aldım" (`src/lib/cardView.ts` `words()` / `TICKET_MODES || kind === "activity"`), and counts as a
    "Rezerve et" to-do with a ticket deadline (`src/lib/progress.ts:156,185,231` `BOOK_CATEGORIES` includes
    "activity"). That is why the Douro card shows "⏳ Bilet alınmadı · etkinliğe 4 gün". A market visit would
    look and count the same. There is no field for it: `Item` (`src/lib/types.ts:242-300`) has no
    needs-booking flag, no done state and no to-do icon. `PlannedKind` has "activity" and "note" but no "todo".
    Fix: add `Item.booking?: "needed" | "none"`, or a `PlannedKind "todo"` plus `Item.doneAt?: number`. Set it
    in extraction (`src/lib/extract.ts` schema: "giriş bileti/rezervasyon gerekiyor mu"), in the chat tool
    (`src/lib/assistant.ts` plan_item: "pazara gitmek" → todo) and in the add sheet (a "Yapılacak" tile).
    Keep `booking: "none"` items off the Plan front and out of `BOOK_CATEGORIES`, and show them in Fikirler (#20).
20. **MISSING (complaint 5): no Fikirler view.** The tabs are only "Plan" and "Günlük akış"
    (`src/app/TripPanel.tsx:349-355`; `dom-facts.json` "view tabs"). `TimelineMode` is `"plan" | "days"`
    (`src/app/Timeline.tsx`). Everything in the mockup is missing:
    - header "Fikirler ve yapılacaklar · rezervasyon gerekmez" and its line;
    - quick-add input "Bir fikir yaz…";
    - filters Hepsi / Yeme-içme / Yapılacaklar;
    - per-city groups with date range ("Porto 7–10 Ekim");
    - restaurant photo cards 196×120 with "ad / mutfak · €€ · ★" and "+ Güne ekle" / a set day chip
      ("8 Eki akşam");
    - a map button;
    - the to-do checklist with check circle, type icon (camera, route, book, sun, bag), title + grey sub line
      and day chip;
    - done state (green check, strike-through, "Yapıldı · 12 Eki");
    - "Rezerve edileceklere taşı" for one that turns out to need a ticket.
    Fix: add `TimelineMode "ideas"` and a third tab. Build `IdeasView` from saved food items, `booking: "none"`
    activities and notes, grouped by `plan.stayBlocks` city. "+ Güne ekle" sets `dates.start` (+ optional meal
    slot) so the item shows as a thin line in Günlük akış. The map button uses `routeUrl` / `items.ts:465`
    maps search with `geo` or name + city. "Rezerve edileceklere taşı" flips `booking` to "needed".
21. **DIFFERENT (complaint 5): restaurants today are bookable decision cards in a carousel under the plan.**
    Undated / saved food sits in a collapsed "Yeme-içme" row below the trip line. Opened, it shows a
    full-width media card "Restoran · Karar bekliyor · Plana seç" (`1440-14-section-Yeme-içme.png`). A chosen
    dated restaurant becomes an "event" block on the Plan front with "Rezerve ettim"
    (`src/lib/timeline.ts:422`, `cardView.ts` `reserveWords`). Saved dated food appears in Günlük akış only as a
    grey "Fikirler" count line (`src/lib/journey.ts:409-410`). Undated food doesn't appear there at all
    (`1440-12-days.png`). There are no photos ("fotoğraflar haritadan" is not built; the spec defers Google
    Places), no cuisine or price level (no field in `ItemMetrics`) and no map button. Fix: with #20, move
    food and to-dos out of `SUMMARIZED` sections on Plan (`TripPanel.tsx:76, 376-385`) into Fikirler. Keep only
    restaurants with a reservation (booking "needed") as Plan cards.
22. **DIFFERENT: undated activities show as a horizontal carousel of big decision cards.** "Etkinlikler" opens
    Serralves / Livraria Lello / Tiyatro as 1/3 carousel cards with "Plana seç" (`1440-14-section-Etkinlikler.png`).
    In fikirler-v1, Livraria Lello is a checklist row with "Giriş bileti gerekiyor · Rezerve edileceklere taşı".
    Fix: after #19, bookable ones stay in a "Rezerve edilecekler" list (the etkinlik-v4 section header
    "Rezerve edilecekler · 5 · 2 alındı" is also not built). Non-bookable ones go to Fikirler.
